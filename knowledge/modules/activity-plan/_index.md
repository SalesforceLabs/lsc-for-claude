# Activity Plan

Activity Plans turn provider interaction strategy into actionable tasks with defined goals and targets for field reps. The plan moves through a lifecycle (admin setup → review → execution), with goal counts tracked at Account, Goal Measure, and Goal Measure Products layers.

## Plan Types

| Type | Territory cardinality |
|---|---|
| **Account Goal** | Single territory |
| **Weighted Territory Goals** | Single territory |
| **Account Goal Shared** | Up to 10 territories |

All three types support both Non-Product and Product measure types.
